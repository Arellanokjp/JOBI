# JOBI — Job Opportunities, Better Careers

### Connecting Talent with Opportunity.

JOBI is a recruitment platform designed to connect job seekers with employers, simplify the recruitment process, and make finding suitable employment opportunities easier.

The platform provides a centralized place where job seekers can discover jobs and apply for them, employers can recruit qualified candidates, and administrators can oversee platform activities and verify employers.

## About JOBI

Finding suitable employment and recruiting qualified candidates can be challenging. JOBI aims to make both processes easier by bringing job seekers and employers together on one platform.

The platform focuses on accessibility, trust, efficient recruitment, and a user-friendly experience.

## User Roles

JOBI will support three main user roles:

### 1. Job Seekers

Job seekers will be able to:

- Create an account and verify their email address.
- Log in securely and manage their profiles.
- Upload and manage one professional resume.
- Browse available job opportunities.
- Search and filter jobs according to relevant criteria.
- View job descriptions and employer information.
- Apply for suitable job opportunities.
- Track the status of submitted applications.
- Save jobs they are interested in.
- Receive notifications about applications and other important activities.
- Manage their account and password securely.

### 2. Employers

Employers will be able to:

- Register an employer account and verify their email address.
- Create and manage company profiles.
- Submit business information and supporting documents for verification.
- Receive administrator approval before accessing restricted recruitment features.
- Create, publish, edit, and manage job vacancies once approved.
- View applications submitted to their vacancies.
- Review applicants' resumes and qualifications.
- Track and manage the recruitment process.
- Update application statuses.
- Manage job listings and recruitment activities.

Employer email verification and company approval will be separate processes. Verifying an email address will not automatically approve an employer.

### 3. Administrators

Administrators will oversee the platform and maintain its integrity.

They will be able to:

- Access a protected administration area.
- Manage user accounts.
- Review employer and company verification requests.
- Examine submitted business documents.
- Approve or reject employer verification requests.
- Manage account statuses.
- Monitor job listings and applications.
- Review relevant platform activity.
- Take appropriate action against suspicious or inappropriate activity.
- Help maintain a safe and trustworthy recruitment environment.

Administrative capabilities will be restricted to authorized users.

## Job Opportunities and Recruitment

JOBI will provide job management and recruitment functionality that allows employers and job seekers to interact through structured application workflows.

The platform will support:

- Publishing job vacancies.
- Browsing available opportunities.
- Searching and filtering job listings.
- Viewing job requirements and descriptions.
- Applying for vacancies.
- Managing submitted applications.
- Reviewing candidates.
- Tracking application progress.
- Managing recruitment decisions.

Only eligible, approved employers will be permitted to publish job vacancies.

## Resume Management

Job seekers will be able to maintain a professional resume on their account.

The resume functionality will support:

- Uploading and managing one resume per job seeker.
- Making relevant qualifications and experience available during applications.
- Allowing authorized employers to review resumes associated with their applications.

Access to resumes and personal information will be restricted appropriately.

## Application Tracking

JOBI will help job seekers and employers follow the recruitment process.

Job seekers will be able to review their application history and check application statuses.

Employers will be able to review applications, assess candidates, and update recruitment progress.

This will help both parties stay informed throughout the hiring process.

## Notifications

The platform will provide notifications for important account and recruitment activities, including:

- Email verification.
- Password recovery and account security.
- Application submissions.
- Application status updates.
- Interview scheduling.
- Important account notifications.

These notifications will help users stay informed about activities relevant to them.

## Account Security

JOBI will incorporate security measures designed to protect user accounts and personal information.

These will include:

- Email verification during registration.
- Secure authentication.
- Password hashing.
- OTP-based password recovery and password changes.
- Role-based access permissions.
- Account-status checks.
- Protected administrative functions.
- Separate employer verification and approval processes.

## AI-Powered Recruitment

JOBI is intended to incorporate AI-assisted features that improve the job search and recruitment experience.

Planned capabilities include:

### Intelligent Job Matching
Recommend job opportunities based on a job seeker's skills, qualifications, experience, and career interests.

### Resume Analysis
Analyze resumes to identify relevant skills, qualifications, and experience.

### Candidate Insights
Help employers organize and review applicant information against job requirements.

### Fraud Detection
Help identify suspicious job listings and potentially fraudulent recruitment activity.

These features are intended to support users and improve recruitment efficiency, rather than replace appropriate human judgment.

## Privacy and Trust

JOBI is designed to provide a recruitment environment where users can interact with greater confidence.

The platform will emphasize:

- Protection of personal information.
- Appropriate access to resumes and applications.
- Employer verification.
- Administrative oversight.
- Secure account management.
- Responsible handling of recruitment information.

## Our Mission

To make employment opportunities more accessible and recruitment more efficient by connecting job seekers with employers through a trusted digital platform.

## Our Vision

To become a reliable recruitment platform that helps people discover opportunities, develop their careers, and connect with employers seeking talent.
